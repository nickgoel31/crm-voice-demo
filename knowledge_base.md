# I.T.S Engineering College, Ghaziabad - Knowledge Base

## About the College
I.T.S Engineering College, Greater Noida (Ghaziabad region), is a premier engineering institution. It is part of the I.T.S Education Group.

## Courses Offered
1. **B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology)**
   - Computer Science & Engineering
   - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
   - Mechanical Engineering
   - Civil Engineering
   - Electronics & Communication
2. **MBA (Master of Business Administration)**

## Admission Process
- Admissions are based on JEE Main scores and UPSEE counseling.
- Management quota seats are also available for meritorious students.

## Placement Highlights
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, HCL, and many more.
- Average Package: ₹4.5 - ₹6 LPA.
- Highest Package: ₹12 LPA+.

## Infrastructure
- State-of-the-art labs.
- Apple iOS Lab for mobile app development.
- Innovation and Incubation Center (supported by MSME).
